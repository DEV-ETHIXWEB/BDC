## Things the articles deliberately do not say

Because the site does not state them, and guessing on any of these would put wrong information in
front of a paying guest:

- What the weather will be, or what to wear beyond layers.
- Whether food or drink is provided.
- Your cancellation, deposit or refund terms.
- That crabbing uses pots. True of crabbing generally, but your site does not say it, so the
  article does not either.
- Which boat goes out for a given group size. The article says to confirm it with you.
