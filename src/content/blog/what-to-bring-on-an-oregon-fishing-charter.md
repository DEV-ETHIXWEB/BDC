The short answer: not much. The guide, the boat, and all fishing gear and bait are included
on every fishing trip, and crab charters include the guide, the boat and all necessary
equipment. You are welcome to bring your own rod if you would rather fish with it.

There is one thing nobody can bring for you, and it is the one thing that will stop your day
before it starts.

## Your Oregon fishing licence

An Oregon fishing licence is required for anyone aged 12 or older. Every angler, whatever
their age, needs a valid angling tag and has to follow the rules on recording harvest.

On top of that, every angler of any age must have a valid Columbia River Basin Endorsement in
their possession when fishing for salmon, steelhead or sturgeon in the mainstem Columbia, and
in every river and tributary that flows into it. That covers most of the water these trips
run on.

Licences come from the Oregon Department of Fish and Wildlife, and you can buy one online
before you travel. Sort it out at home rather than at 4am on the way to the launch.

## Dress for an early start

Trips can begin as early as 4:00 AM, which is most common for salmon, when the first light is
the most productive part of the day. Start times move with the season and the species, and
Captain Clinton confirms yours when you book.

That means you will be on the water through the coldest part of the morning, in a season that
may be winter. Winter steelhead runs January through April, spring and early Chinook March
through July, and fall salmon August through November. Crab charters run October through
December. Layers you can peel off as the sun comes up will serve you better than one heavy
coat.

## Something to carry your fish home in

If you keep fish, you need a way to get it home. A cooler in the car is the simple answer,
and it is worth putting it in the boot the night before.

## Know your group size

Group size depends on the boat. The Willy Predator takes up to 6 anglers, though it is often
limited to 4 for comfort, and the Alumaweld drift boat takes up to 3 guests for a more
personal trip. Worth confirming the number in your party when you book, because it decides
which boat goes out.

## Where you will be fishing

The exact meeting spot and launch time come from Captain Clinton when you book, because both
change with the season and the river. Trips run on several Oregon rivers, including the
Columbia, Willamette, Wilson, Trask, Sandy, Clackamas and Nestucca, and the choice depends on
the season and where fish are actively biting.

Call or email and he will confirm the details with you.
