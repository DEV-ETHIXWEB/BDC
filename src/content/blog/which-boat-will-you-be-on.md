Two boats, and the difference between them matters more than you would expect. It changes how
many people can come, where you can fish, and what you are likely to catch.

## The 22 foot Willy Predator

The bigger of the two. It seats up to 6 anglers, though it is often limited to 4 so nobody is
fishing elbow to elbow.

This is the boat for Chinook salmon, steelhead and the seasonal species, on the larger water.
Full day is 8 hours at $250 per person, half day is 4 hours at $150. Crabbing on the Predator
is a 5 hour trip at $150 per person, booked for up to 5.

## The 16 foot Alumaweld drift boat

Smaller, and built for shallow water the Predator cannot reach. It takes up to 3 guests, which
makes for a more personal trip.

Winter steelhead is what the drift boat does best, on the river systems where it has the
advantage. Full day is 8 hours at $250 per person, half day is 4 hours at $150, and crabbing
runs 5 hours at $150 for up to 3.

## So which one

Mostly it comes down to two questions.

**How many of you are there?** Four or more and you are on the Predator. Three or fewer and
either boat works.

**What do you want to catch?** Winter steelhead on a quiet river points to the drift boat.
Chinook on bigger water points to the Predator.

Tell Captain Clinton your group size and what you are after when you book, and he will put you
on the right one. He gives you the meeting spot and the launch time then too, because both
change with the season and the river.
