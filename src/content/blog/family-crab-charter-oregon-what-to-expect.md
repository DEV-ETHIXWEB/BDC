An Oregon Dungeness crab charter is one of the easiest ways for a family to spend a few hours on the water. There is no casting to get right and no quiet waiting for a bite. Pots go down, you enjoy the ride, and everyone gathers around when it is time to see what came up.

## When crab season runs

BDC Guide Service runs [Dungeness crab charters](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/oregon-crabbing-charter-bdc-guide-service) from October through December. If a crab trip has been on your list, book early, because dates fill as the season goes on.

## What is included

Every crabbing charter includes the guide, the boat and all the equipment you need, so you do not have to own any gear or know how to bait a pot. Trips run about 5 hours and cost $150 per person.

You go out on one of two boats, depending on your group:

- The [22 foot Willie Predator](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/oregon-crabbing-charter-bdc-guide-service), for up to 5 guests.
- The [16 foot Alumaweld](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/oregon-crabbing-charter-5-hour-adventure), for up to 3 guests.

Not sure which fits? Our guide to [which boat you will be on](/article/which-boat-will-you-be-on) explains the difference.

## What happens on the water

A crab trip is hands-on, which is why it works so well for kids. The pots go down, they soak, and then you pull them up and sort through the catch together. You measure, check and decide what goes in the bucket and what goes back.

That sorting matters. A keeper is a male Dungeness crab at least 5¾ inches across the shell. Females always go back, whatever their size. Your guide helps you tell the difference on the boat, so nobody needs to study first.

## Is it good for kids

Crabbing is probably the easiest trip BDC runs for a family. Something is always happening, and everyone can help. Under 12 do not need a license. Tell Captain Clinton the ages of everyone coming when you book, so he can plan the day around your group.

## The license to sort out first

Oregon requires a shellfish license to keep crab, and it is separate from a fishing license. Everyone 12 or older needs one. Kids under 12 do not. If you are only crabbing, the shellfish license is the only one you need, and you can buy it from [ODFW](https://myodfw.com/fishing/licensing-info) or a license agent. Our [Oregon crabbing rules guide](/article/oregon-crabbing-rules) covers limits and the ocean closure, and our [2026 license and cost guide](/article/oregon-fishing-license-guided-trip) has the prices.

## What to bring

October through December on the Oregon coast means layers. Bring a warm jacket, something waterproof, shoes that can get wet, and food and drink. Captain Clinton confirms your meeting spot and launch time when you book, since it can change with the season and the tides.

Call Captain Clinton at (503) 826-7294 or request a trip on the [trips and rates page](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates). Tell him your group size and he will match you with the right boat.
