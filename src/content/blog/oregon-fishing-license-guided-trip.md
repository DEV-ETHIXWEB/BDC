A guided trip should start with the right paperwork. BDC Guide Service provides the guide, the boat and all the gear. You bring your own license, tags and endorsements, and what you need depends on your age, what you are fishing for and where.

This guide covers the rules and the 2026 prices. For the river by river breakdown, see our [Oregon fishing license guide](/article/get-your-valid-oregon-fishing-license).

## Who needs an Oregon fishing license

Anyone 12 or older needs an Oregon angling license. Kids 12 to 17 can buy the $10 Youth License, which covers angling and shellfish. Children under 12 do not need a standard fishing or shellfish license.

Being under 12 does not remove every requirement. Anglers of any age need a Combined Angling Tag when fishing for salmon, steelhead, sturgeon or Pacific halibut. The youth tag is $5.

## Which tags and endorsements you might need

- **Combined Angling Tag:** needed for salmon, steelhead, sturgeon and Pacific halibut. The adult tag is $69 for Oregon residents and $89 for nonresidents. Record any covered fish you keep the way ODFW instructs.
- **Columbia River Basin Endorsement (CRBE):** needed for salmon, steelhead and sturgeon in the Columbia River and the rivers that flow into it. It is $9.75 with an annual license, $11.75 bought separately, or $1 per day with a daily license.
- **Ocean Endorsement:** needed for fishing in the ocean, unless you are only keeping salmon or steelhead. It is $9 for the year or $4 for a day. It is not needed to take shellfish.
- **Shellfish license:** needed by anyone 12 or older who harvests Dungeness crab, clams or other shellfish.

## Which BDC rivers need the Columbia River Basin Endorsement

The CRBE follows the river system and the species, not how close the river is to the coast.

- **Needed** for salmon, steelhead and sturgeon on the Columbia, Willamette, Sandy and Clackamas rivers.
- **Not needed** on the coastal Wilson, Trask and Nestucca rivers, which run to Tillamook Bay and Nestucca Bay instead of the Columbia.

Not sure which river you will be on? Check our [river guides](/article/columbia-river-fishing-guide) or ask Captain Clinton when you book.

## Going on a crab charter

A crab-only trip has the shortest checklist. Everyone 12 or older needs a shellfish license, and that is all. You do not need an angling license, a Combined Angling Tag or an Ocean Endorsement just to take crab.

BDC runs five hour Dungeness crab charters from October through December at $150 per person. Our [Oregon crabbing rules guide](/article/oregon-crabbing-rules) covers limits and how to measure a keeper. If you plan to fish for finfish on the same day, ask Captain Clinton what else you need.

## What an Oregon fishing license costs in 2026

**Annual**

| Document | Resident | Nonresident |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Angling license | $50 | $138 |
| Combined Angling Tag | $69 | $89 |
| Shellfish license | $13 | $37 |

**Short term**

- One day angling and shellfish combo: $29
- Two day angling license: $48
- Three day angling license: $68
- Seven day angling license (nonresident): $117
- Three day shellfish license (nonresident): $25

Combined Angling Tags are included with daily and multi-day licenses. Add the CRBE at $1 per day or the Ocean Endorsement at $4 per day if your trip needs them.

Fees change, so check [ODFW licensing](https://myodfw.com/fishing/licensing-info) before you buy, and select every document that applies to your trip.

## Before you board: a quick checklist

- Confirm your target species and river with Captain Clinton.
- Check that every guest has the license, tag and endorsements that trip needs.
- Buy through ODFW or an authorized license agent, ahead of the trip.
- Keep your documents, paper or electronic, with you on the boat.

BDC supplies the boat and gear, and guests handle the paperwork. To confirm what your charter needs, call Captain Clinton at (503) 826-7294 or see [trips and rates](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates).
