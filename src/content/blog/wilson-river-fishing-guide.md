The Wilson is a coastal winter steelhead river. It runs west out of the Coast Range to Tillamook Bay, with Highway 6 following much of it, which makes it an easy drive from Portland to the coast.

## When to fish the Wilson River

Winter steelhead run January through April on the coastal and tributary rivers.

## Which trip and boat

Steelhead trips on this river suit the [16 foot Alumaweld drift boat](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/oregon-fishing-charter-full-day-drift-trip), which takes up to 3 guests. A full day is 8 hours at $250 per person and a [half day](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/oregon-fishing-charter-half-day-drift-boat-trip) is 4 hours at $150. Rods, reels, tackle and bait are included.

## License you need

This is a coastal river that does not flow into the Columbia, so the Columbia River Basin Endorsement rule does not cover it. You need an Oregon fishing license and a Combined Angling Tag. Our [license guide](/article/get-your-valid-oregon-fishing-license) explains both.

## Plan your trip

Runs shift with the river and the year, so call Captain Clinton at (503) 826-7294 before you plan around a date. You can see every trip on the [trips and rates page](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates).
