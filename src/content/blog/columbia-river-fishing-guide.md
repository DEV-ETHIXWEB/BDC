Captain Clinton describes the Columbia as big-water Chinook trolling. It is Oregon's largest river, forming most of the border with Washington and running past Portland on its way to the Pacific at Astoria.

## When to fish the Columbia River

Spring and early Chinook run March through July on the Columbia and Willamette.

Fall salmon run August through November, with fall Chinook itself August through October.

American shad and sturgeon are target species on the Columbia too. Sturgeon is catch and release for most of the year, with retention opening only for short announced periods.

## Which trip and boat

Salmon trips here run on the [22 foot Willie Predator](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/full-day-oregon-river-fishing-charter), which seats up to 6 anglers and is often limited to 4 for comfort. A full day is 8 hours at $250 per person and a [half day](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/oregon-fishing-charter-half-day-trip) is 4 hours at $150. Rods, reels, tackle and bait are included.

## License you need

This river is part of the Columbia basin, so for salmon, steelhead or sturgeon you need an Oregon fishing license, a Combined Angling Tag and a Columbia River Basin Endorsement. Our [license guide](/article/get-your-valid-oregon-fishing-license) explains each one.

## Plan your trip

Runs shift with the river and the year, so call Captain Clinton at (503) 826-7294 before you plan around a date. You can see every trip on the [trips and rates page](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates).
