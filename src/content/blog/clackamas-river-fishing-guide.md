The Clackamas gives you both steelhead and salmon, close to Portland. It comes down from the Mt. Hood area and joins the Willamette at Oregon City, which puts it within easy reach of the city.

## When to fish the Clackamas River

Winter steelhead run January through April on the coastal and tributary rivers.

Fall salmon run August through November, with fall Chinook itself August through October.

## Which trip and boat

Steelhead trips on this river suit the [16 foot Alumaweld drift boat](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/oregon-fishing-charter-full-day-drift-trip), which takes up to 3 guests. A full day is 8 hours at $250 per person and a [half day](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/oregon-fishing-charter-half-day-drift-boat-trip) is 4 hours at $150. Rods, reels, tackle and bait are included. For salmon, ask about the [Willie Predator](/oregon-fishing-charter-rates/full-day-oregon-river-fishing-charter).

## License you need

This river is part of the Columbia basin, so for salmon, steelhead or sturgeon you need an Oregon fishing license, a Combined Angling Tag and a Columbia River Basin Endorsement. Our [license guide](/article/get-your-valid-oregon-fishing-license) explains each one.
