THE FISHING YEAR
----------------
Winter steelhead         Jan - Apr
Spring & early Chinook   Mar - Jul
Fall salmon              Aug - Nov
American shad            Early summer
Sturgeon                 Catch & release, all year
Dungeness crab           Oct - Dec

The season opens August 1. Runs shift with the river and the year, so
Captain Clinton will tell you what is really happening the week you fish.

BEFORE YOU COME OUT
-------------------
* Everyone 12 and older needs an Oregon fishing licence:
  https://www.bdcguideservices.com/article/get-your-valid-oregon-fishing-license
* Rods, reels, bait and tackle are included. Bring your own gear only if
  you want to.
